Sourced vs influenced

Sourced means the channel created the deal. Influenced means the buyer engaged with it after the deal was already open, like an Enterprise prospect attending re:Invent mid-cycle. One deal can be influenced by several channels, so influenced totals overlap and shouldn't be added up.
